## 2. Datenschutzerklärung (Privacy Policy)
*(Pflichtangabe im Chrome Web Store)*

```markdown
# Privacy Policy for AdShredder by Mojo

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

AdShredder by Mojo ("the Extension") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed and how it is handled.

1. Data Collection
AdShredder by Mojo does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, browsing history, IP addresses, or identifiable user information. 

2. Permissions Used
• declarativeNetRequest / declarativeNetRequestFeedback: Used strictly to block known advertising and tracker network requests locally on your device.
• storage: Used strictly to save your local preferences (custom hidden CSS selectors, whitelist, master switch status, and local counters). This data never leaves your device.
• activeTab / tabs: Used strictly to apply cosmetic rules to the current active webpage and display domain statistics in the popup.

3. Third Parties
The Extension does not communicate with any external analytics, marketing, or advertising servers. All filtering is executed entirely within your browser.

4. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact Mojo via the project repository.
```
